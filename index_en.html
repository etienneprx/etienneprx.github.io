About

Master's student in Political Science at Université Laval, my research interests focus on AI governance and the influence of industry actors in legislative debates. My doctoral research project aims to combine critical discourse analysis and computational methods to examine how the notion of public interest is constructed in AI policy across Canada, Europe, and the United States.

Trained in digital social sciences, I develop in R and use mixed methods (qualitative and quantitative). I have contributed to publications in Social Science Computer Review and collaborate with the Chair for Leadership in Teaching Digital Social Sciences at Université Laval.

Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of political science, communication studies, and digital social sciences. I am particularly interested in:

  • Artificial intelligence governance and regulation
  • Influence of industry actors in legislative processes
  • Critical discourse analysis and construction of public interest
  • Computational methods and mixed approaches in social sciences
